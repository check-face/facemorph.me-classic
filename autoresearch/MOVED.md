# Research has moved

Since 24 October 2026 the FaceMorph research home is the monorepo,
[check-face/facemorph.me](https://github.com/check-face/facemorph.me), from commit
[`8f6369a`](https://github.com/check-face/facemorph.me/commit/8f6369a7a7a54fb44b6760010b338cdb3a8cf082) (bead fm-zxj).

- **Start no new round here.** This directory is history: its rows, logs, candidates and reports stay as they are.
- **Canonical ledger:** [autoresearch/results.tsv](https://github.com/check-face/facemorph.me/blob/main/autoresearch/results.tsv)
  in the monorepo. It begins with this ledger's bytes as imported from `b2d945a`, and its CI fails if a row is appended
  here after the handover.
- **Command journal:** the machine's shared lease journal, `<lease dir>/runs.jsonl` (see
  [ops/lease](https://github.com/check-face/facemorph.me/blob/main/ops/lease/README.md)); both repositories' runs use it.
- **Guide and protocol:** [autoresearch/README.md](https://github.com/check-face/facemorph.me/blob/main/autoresearch/README.md)
  and [autoresearch/program.md](https://github.com/check-face/facemorph.me/blob/main/autoresearch/program.md) in the monorepo.
- **Handover record:** [rounds/2026-10-24-handover](https://github.com/check-face/facemorph.me/blob/main/autoresearch/rounds/2026-10-24-handover/README.md).
